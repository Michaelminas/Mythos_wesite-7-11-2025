# Founders story

Source: Terry's first-person account supplied on 18 October 2026.

Terry and Anton have been friends since primary school. MYTHOS began with a conversation on the drive home from the gym about creating their own party. Shared energy, community and Greek heritage underpin the business. Terry described feeling distant from family in Greece; Anton brings an outgoing spirit and connection to the Greek community. The name represents story or legend and experiences remembered beyond the celebration.

Terry: creative direction, marketing, branding and overall event vision.
Anton: entertainment, logistics, supplier relationships and delivery on the day.
Both: planning and decision-making.

Private planning followed their enjoyment of creating MYTHOS events and the supplier/venue/entertainment relationships built along the way. The published story paraphrases this account, without invented quotes, dates, credentials or biographical details.
